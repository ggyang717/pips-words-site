How do children play?

Pick a place on the map. Pip says a word; find that thing in the picture and tap it. Tapping anything else also says its name, so there is nothing to fail. Find all 12 words and the scene comes alive with a short story, and the stickers go into the sticker book.

How do I change the language my child is learning?

On the map screen, tap the gear button → I want to learn → English, Español or 日本語. Menus and translations follow your iPhone's language; you can choose the translation language in the same menu (Translation language). When the learning language is the same as the translation language, translations are hidden.

What is free, and what can I buy?

All 8 places on the first map (Word Town) are free. Each other map can be unlocked on its own, or you can choose Unlock All once, which also includes maps added in future updates. All purchases are one-time; there are no subscriptions and no ads. Prices are shown in the app by the App Store.

Parental gate

Before any purchase screen or sharing, the app asks a simple maths question meant for a grown-up. If your child uses Family Sharing with Ask to Buy, purchases wait for your approval and unlock automatically once approved.

Restoring purchases

On a new device, open a locked map (or gear → About → Unlock more maps), answer the parental question, then tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Downloaded content

The free map's pictures, animations and English voice are inside the app. After a purchase, the animations of the unlocked maps download from Apple in the background; Spanish and Japanese voice recordings download when that language is chosen. Until a download finishes, scenes show their still picture and words use the device's built-in voice. After downloading, everything works offline. If a download does not appear, check the internet connection and free storage, then reopen the app.

Sound and replay

Gear → Sound effects / Music turns them on or off. Words are read aloud even when the silent switch is on. To replay a finished place, tap its big Play again button or long-press its name on the map; stickers and badges are kept.

孩子怎么玩？

在地图上选一个地方。皮普说一个词，到画里找到它点一下。点别的东西也会读出名字，所以不会「输」。12 个词都找到后，整幅画会动起来并讲一个小故事，贴纸收进贴纸本。

怎么切换孩子要学的语言？

地图页点齿轮按钮 →「我要学」→ English、Español 或 日本語。界面和释义跟随手机系统语言；同一个菜单里的「释义语言」可以另选。学的语言和释义语言相同时不显示释义。

哪些免费，可以买什么？

第一张地图「Word Town」的 8 个地方全部免费。其他地图可以单张解锁，也可以「全部解锁」一次买齐（以后更新新增的地图也包含）。都是一次性购买，没有订阅，没有广告；价格以 App 内 App Store 显示为准。

家长验证

进入购买页或分享之前，App 会出一道给大人的算术题。如果孩子的账号开了家人共享的「购买前询问」，购买会等您批准，批准后自动解锁。

恢复购买

换了新设备：点一张锁住的地图（或齿轮 →「关于」→「解锁更多地图」），答对家长验证题后点「恢复购买」。请使用当初购买的 Apple 账户。

下载内容

免费地图的图片、动画和英语配音都在 App 里。购买后，已解锁地图的动画会在后台从 Apple 服务器下载；选了西班牙语或日语时会下载对应的配音。下载完成前场景显示静态画面，单词用系统语音朗读；下载完成后可离线使用。如果一直没有下载好，请检查网络和剩余空间，再重新打开 App。

声音与重玩

齿轮 →「音效」/「音乐」可开关。手机静音时也会读单词。想重玩已完成的地方：点场景里的「再玩一次」大按钮，或在地图上长按这个地方的名牌；贴纸和徽章会保留。
