Pip's Words · 皮普学单词

Find it, hear it, learn it.
在画里找一找，边听边学词。

Pip's Words is a calm seek-and-find word game for children (about 6–8). Explore handmade cut-paper scenes, tap anything to hear its name, and find the word Pip asks for. Children can learn English, Spanish or Japanese; the app's menus and translations follow the device language (English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Português).

No ads. No accounts. No data collected. The first map is free; other maps are one-time purchases behind a parental gate.

皮普学单词是一款给 6–8 岁左右孩子的「找一找」学词游戏：走进手工剪纸风的场景，点什么都会读出它的名字，跟着皮普一个一个找到要找的词。可以学英语、西班牙语或日语；界面和释义跟随手机系统语言。

没有广告、没有账号、不收集任何数据。第一张地图免费，其他地图一次性购买，购买前有家长验证。
