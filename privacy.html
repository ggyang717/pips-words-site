In short: Pip's Words does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What your child does in the app stays on the device.

What stays on the device

  • Progress (words found, stickers, passport stamps and badges) and settings (learning language, translation language, sound and music) are saved only on the device, in the app's local storage. We cannot see them and they are never sent to us.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

What we do not do

  • No personal information is collected: no name, age, email, location, contacts, photos, voice or device identifiers.
  • No camera or microphone use, no chat, no user-generated content, no social features.
  • No third-party SDKs: no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code.

Purchases

Optional one-time in-app purchases (a single map or Unlock All) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive payment details or personal information; Apple only tells the app which items have been purchased. Purchase screens are behind a parental gate.

Downloaded content

Some content (animations for purchased maps, and Spanish and Japanese voice recordings) is delivered as Apple On-Demand Resources. These files are hosted and served by Apple; the app does not contact any server run by us or by any third party.

Sharing

A grown-up can share a picture card of the child's stickers or badges. The card is made on the device and handed to the iOS share sheet only after the parental gate. The app does not upload it anywhere.

Children's privacy

Pip's Words is designed for children. Because it does not collect, store on our side, or share any personal information, it is designed to meet the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU/UK GDPR rules for children's data (GDPR-K / Age Appropriate Design Code). There is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; parents can remove all local data by deleting the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: 1091976732@qq.com or the support page.

  • 学习进度（找到的词、贴纸、护照印章和徽章）和设置（要学的语言、释义语言、音效和音乐）只保存在设备上的 App 本地存储里。我们看不到，也不会传给我们。

  • 不收集任何个人信息：姓名、年龄、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、声音、设备标识符都不收集。

购买

可选的一次性 App 内购买（单张地图或「全部解锁」）完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们不会收到付款信息或个人信息，Apple 只告诉 App 买了哪些项目。购买页之前有家长验证。

下载内容

部分内容（已购买地图的动画、西班牙语和日语配音）通过 Apple 的「按需下载资源」（On-Demand Resources）提供，文件由 Apple 托管和分发；App 不连接我们或任何第三方的服务器。

分享

家长可以分享孩子的贴纸或徽章图片卡。图片在设备上生成，过了家长验证后交给 iOS 的分享面板；App 不会上传到任何地方。

儿童隐私

本 App 为儿童设计。由于不收集、不在我们这边存储、也不分享任何个人信息，它按照美国《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA）和欧盟 / 英国 GDPR 关于儿童数据的规定（GDPR-K / 适龄设计规范）设计。我们这里没有任何可供查询、更正或删除的数据；家长删除 App 即可清除全部本地数据。
